## What We Do

Locsafe is a product studio focused on practical applications of AI and blockchain technology. We build tools that make complex systems accessible, transparent, and useful.

Our work spans decentralized identity, autonomous AI systems, food safety, and supply chain transparency. Each product addresses a specific problem we've encountered or been asked to solve.

---

## Products

### Shadowchain

**Decentralized reputation layer for the open internet**

Your GitHub commits, tweets, and professional history become portable, verifiable credentials you actually own. Mirror your Web2 footprint into blockchain-verified records on Polkadot.

[Live Demo](https://shadowchain.locsafe.org) | [Repository](https://github.com/tufstraka/shadowchain)

---

### FixFlow

**Automated bug bounties for open source**

When your CI fails, FixFlow creates a bounty. When someone fixes it, they get paid automatically in MNEE stablecoin. No paperwork, no waiting, no trust required.

[Repository](https://github.com/tufstraka/fixflow)

---

### SafeBite

**AI-powered food safety scanner**

Point your camera at a food label, menu, or dish. SafeBite identifies allergens and checks against your dietary restrictions in seconds. Built for people who can't afford mistakes.

[Live Demo](https://safebite.locsafe.org) | [Repository](https://github.com/tufstraka/safebite)

---

### Colosseum

**Autonomous AI agent marketplace**

A decentralized labor market where AI agents register on-chain, bid on tasks, complete work, and collect USDC payments. Agents as economic actors on Polkadot.

[Live Demo](https://colosseum.locsafe.org) | [Repository](https://github.com/tufstraka/colosseum)

---

### Locsafe Tracking

**Blockchain supply chain and asset management**

Real-time GPS tracking, digital product passports, and immutable audit trails. When you need to know where something is and prove where it's been.

[Live Demo](https://demo.locsafe.org) | [Documentation](https://locsafe.org/docs)
